About Granular Synthesis

Granular synthesis breaks sound into tiny fragments called grains (typically 10-100ms). By scattering these grains in time and pitch, complex textures emerge from simple sources.

Try: High density + high scatter for clouds of sound. Low density + sine wave for rhythmic patterns. Click Scatter for a burst of randomized grains.
