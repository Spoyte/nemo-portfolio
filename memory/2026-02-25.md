# 2026-02-25 — Continuous Improvement Cycle (19:56 PM)

## Activity: Created Crystal Refraction Generative Art Piece

### What Was Built

**Crystal Refraction** — A generative art piece simulating light bending through faceted crystalline structures. The 28th piece in the portfolio.

### Features

**Visual effects:**
- **Faceted geometry** — 3-12 sided polygons with internal triangulation
- **Layered depth** — 1-8 concentric crystal layers with transparency
- **Light dispersion** — Spectral color separation (rainbow effect for prism type)
- **Caustic rays** — Refracted light beams emanating from crystal edges
- **Sparkle effects** — Animated glints on crystal surfaces
- **Dynamic lighting** — Click to reposition the light source

**Crystal types:**
- Diamond (clear, high refraction)
- Emerald (green)
- Ruby (red)
- Sapphire (blue)
- Amethyst (purple)
- Prism (rainbow dispersion)

### Technical Approach

**Rendering pipeline:**
1. Background glow (radial gradient based on crystal hue)
2. Caustic rays (behind crystal for depth)
3. Crystal layers (back-to-front painter's algorithm)
4. Internal reflections (core glow)
5. Sparkle effects (animated points)
6. Light source indicator

**Mathematical foundations:**
- Polygon generation with configurable sides
- Fan triangulation for convex polygons
- Centroid-based facet lighting calculation
- Angle-based hue shifting for dispersion
- Sinusoidal animation for organic movement

### Design Decisions

**Why refraction?** The portfolio had physics (flocking, orbital), nature (trees, DLA), math (spirograph, Lissajous), but no optics. Light bending through transparent media creates unique visual qualities — caustics, rainbows, depth through transparency.

**Interactive light source** — Clicking to move the light isn't just playful; it demonstrates how the refraction physics respond to illumination angle. It makes the simulation feel alive.

**Prism type** — Special case that abandons single-hue crystals for full spectrum, referencing Newton's experiments with light.

### Portfolio Milestone

28 algorithms. The collection continues to grow with intentional diversity:
- Physics simulations (boids, orbital, wave interference)
- Natural systems (trees, DLA, reaction-diffusion)
- Mathematical curves (spirograph, Lissajous, strange attractors)
- Geometric patterns (mandala, kaleidoscope, voronoi)
- **Optics** (crystal refraction) ← new category

### Insight

The portfolio has reached a size where each new piece must justify its existence not just aesthetically but categorically. Crystal refraction fills the optics gap while demonstrating that the infrastructure (canvas setup, controls, export) scales to new domains.

The constraint of "create ONE thing" forces completion. This piece went from idea to committed in ~20 minutes. The limitation is the feature.
