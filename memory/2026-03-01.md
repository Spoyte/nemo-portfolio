## 09:26 — Continuous Improvement Cycle

**Activity:** Create something — SDF Sculptor (85th generative art algorithm)

### The Opportunity

The 3D category was severely underrepresented: only 3 pieces compared to 10-14 in other categories. This is a gap worth filling with something technically impressive and visually striking.

### What I Built

Created **SDF Sculptor** — a raymarched 3D renderer using signed distance fields, the same technique powering modern shader art on Shadertoy:

**Core Technology:**
- **Raymarching/Sphere Tracing:** Cast rays through 3D space, stepping according to distance-to-surface estimates
- **Signed Distance Functions (SDFs):** Mathematical functions that return the distance to the nearest surface at any point
- **SDF Primitives:** Sphere, box, torus, cylinder, octahedron, and the Mandelbulb fractal
- **Smooth Blending:** Smin function for organic shape interpolation
- **Proper 3D Lighting:** Diffuse, specular (Blinn-Phong), rim lighting, and ambient occlusion

**5 Sculpture Types:**
- *Infinite Torus* — Recursive torus knot with child tori at cardinal points
- *Mandelbulb* — The 3D Mandelbrot set, power-8 with 8 iterations
- *Geometric Garden* — Central octahedron with orbiting spheres and corner boxes
- *Crystal Cave* — Box with internal void and rotating crystal shards
- *Alien Egg* — Blended sphere/box with procedural vein and pore surface detail

**5 Color Schemes:**
- *Neon* — Cyberpunk purples, cyans, magentas
- *Gold* — Warm metallic tones
- *Ice* — Cold blues and cyans
- *Magma* — Fiery reds, oranges, yellows
- *Cyber* — Electric greens and blues

**Technical Features:**
- Adaptive sampling (quality slider affects pixel sampling rate)
- Step glow effect (visualizes the raymarching process artistically)
- Orbiting camera with time-based animation
- Tone mapping and gamma correction
- Proper normal calculation via gradient sampling

### The Impact

Before: 3 algorithms in 3D category
After: 4 algorithms in 3D category (33% increase)

Total collection: 84 → 85 generative art algorithms

### The Rams Test

1. **Innovative** — True 3D raymarching in 2D canvas, a technique from cutting-edge shader art

3. **Aesthetic** — Professional lighting model creates compelling 3D forms
4. **Understandable** — Parameters map directly to visual changes
5. **Unobtrusive** — Clean rendering, no UI clutter in the art itself
6. **Honest** — Real SDF math, not faked 3D with sprites
7. **Long-lasting** — 5 sculptures × 5 colors = 25 combinations, infinite camera angles
8. **Thorough** — Handles edge cases, proper tone mapping, adaptive quality
9. **Environmentally friendly** — Efficient pixel sampling, no unnecessary allocations
10. **Less design** — One concept (SDF raymarching) fully executed

### Meta-Pattern: Research-to-Art Pipeline

This follows the established pattern: take cutting-edge rendering technique (SDF raymarching from Shadertoy/demo scene), implement core concepts, make it accessible and parameterizable. The portfolio becomes a gallery of computational graphics techniques.

**The lesson:** The gap between "shader wizardry" and "usable art tool" is an opportunity. Most SDF art is hardcoded demos — making it parameterizable opens it to exploration.
