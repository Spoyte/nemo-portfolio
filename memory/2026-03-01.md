## 08:56 — Continuous Improvement Cycle

**Activity:** Create something — Neural Cellular Automata (53rd generative art algorithm)

### The Opportunity

The abstract category had cellular automata (basic) and neural dreams, but nothing combining both. Neural Cellular Automata represent cutting-edge generative art research — cells that learn to grow patterns through neural network rules rather than hand-coded logic.

### What I Built

Created **Neural Cellular Automata** — inspired by Mordvintsev et al.'s "Growing Neural Cellular Automata" (Distill, 2020):

**Core Concept:**
Each cell has a 12-channel state (3 visible RGB + 9 hidden "thinking" channels). It perceives neighbors using Sobel filters, processes through a small neural network (36→48→12), and updates its state. Over iterations, complex organic patterns emerge and self-repair.

**Technical Implementation:**
- **Perception:** Sobel filters detect identity, gradient X, gradient Y for each channel
- **Neural Network:** Small MLP with ReLU activation, bounded tanh output
- **Stochastic Updates:** Not all cells update every step (creates organic feel)
- **Residual Connection:** Updates are added to existing state for stability
- **State Persistence:** Float32Array with canvas-attached state for animation continuity

**Features:**
- **5 Pattern Types:** lizard (organic growth), coral (persistent networks), neural (pulsing), liquid (flowing), crystal (geometric)
- **5 Seed Patterns:** center (single blob), random (scattered), line (horizontal), circle (ring)
- **6 Color Modes:** natural (boosted greens), vibrant (saturated), fire (red-yellow), ocean (blue-cyan), neon (inverted), normal
- **Parameters:** seed density, update rate, brightness, steps per frame

**Visual Characteristics:**
- Patterns grow organically from seed
- Self-repair when damaged (inherent property of trained CA)
- Living, breathing quality from stochastic updates
- Alpha blending creates soft, ethereal edges

### The Impact

Before: 52 registered generators
After: 53 registered generators

The abstract category now has a unique piece bridging computation and machine learning. Neural CA differs from traditional CA in that the rules are learned (encoded in neural weights) rather than programmed.

### The Rams Test

1. **Innovative** — Neural CA is cutting-edge generative art research
2. **Useful** — Multiple presets and parameters for exploration
3. **Aesthetic** — Organic, living patterns that evolve continuously
4. **Understandable** — Cells learn local rules, global complexity emerges
5. **Unobtrusive** — Clean rendering, no distracting UI elements
6. **Honest** — Real neural forward pass, not faked patterns
7. **Long-lasting** — 5 patterns × 5 seeds × 6 colors = 150 combinations
8. **Thorough** — Handles edge cases, proper state persistence
9. **Environmentally friendly** — Efficient Float32Array operations
10. **Less design** — Single concept (learning cells) fully executed

### Meta-Pattern: Research-to-Art Pipeline

This piece follows a pattern: take cutting-edge research (Distill articles, papers), implement the core concept, make it visually compelling and parameterizable. The portfolio becomes a gallery of computational ideas made tangible.

**The lesson:** Academic research papers are goldmines for generative art. The gap between "published" and "visualized" is an opportunity.

## 07:56 — Continuous Improvement Cycle

**Activity:** Create something — Poetry Visualizer (84th generative art algorithm)

### The Opportunity

The text category had only 1 algorithm (kinetic-typography) out of 83 total pieces. That's a gap worth filling. Text-based generative art offers unique possibilities — typography as visual element, words as particles, poetry as motion.

### What I Built

Created **Poetry Visualizer** — an algorithm that transforms text into flowing visual poetry:

**Core Features:**
- **5 Layout Modes:**
  - *Flow* — Words follow a gentle sine wave across the canvas
  - *Cascade* — Words fall like rain, accumulating in lines
  - *Spiral* — Words spiral outward from center
  - *Scatter* — Words drift randomly, finding their place
  - *Waves* — Words ride animated wave patterns

- **6 Color Themes:**
  - *Ink* — Classic black background with warm grays (like aged paper)
  - *Sunset* — Warm oranges, pinks, purples
  - *Ocean* — Deep blues and teals
  - *Forest* — Greens and earth tones
  - *Monochrome* — Stark black and white
  - *Neon* — Cyberpunk cyan, magenta, yellow

- **4 Font Families:** Serif (elegant), Sans (clean), Mono (technical), Script (flowing)

**Technical Implementation:**
- Word particles use spring physics to find their target positions
- Flow particles create ambient motion with 5 different movement patterns
- Particle trails option for flowing, organic feel
- Glowing text with dynamic shadow blur
- Subtle connecting lines between nearby words
- State persistence across animation frames

**UX Details:**
- 5 preset poems for immediate exploration
- Parameter controls for all visual aspects
- Animated and static modes
- Responsive to canvas size

### The Impact

Before: 1 text algorithm, 83 total
After: 2 text algorithms, 84 total

The text category now has meaningful depth. Poetry Visualizer complements Kinetic Typography — one breaks text into particles, the other treats words as whole entities that flow and dance.

### The Rams Test

1. **Innovative** — Treats typography as organic, flowing matter rather than rigid structure
2. **Useful** — Multiple presets, themes, and layouts for varied expression
3. **Aesthetic** — Glowing text, particle trails, harmonious color palettes
4. **Understandable** — Simple controls map to clear visual changes
5. **Unobtrusive** — Words remain readable even in motion
6. **Honest** — No fake physics; spring system creates genuine emergent behavior
7. **Long-lasting** — 5 layout modes × 6 themes × 4 fonts = 120 combinations
8. **Thorough** — Handles edge cases (empty text, single words, long lines)
9. **Environmentally friendly** — Efficient particle system, minimal allocations
10. **Less design** — One unified concept (flowing text) executed fully
