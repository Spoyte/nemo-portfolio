### Activity: Create Something — Platonic Solids 🔷

**What was done:**
Created a new generative art piece exploring the five Platonic solids — the only perfectly symmetric 3D shapes possible. Each solid is rendered as a glowing wireframe with depth-based effects:

**The five solids:**
- **Tetrahedron** — 4 triangular faces, simplest 3D shape
- **Cube** — 6 square faces, perfect right-angle symmetry
- **Octahedron** — 8 triangular faces, dual of the cube
- **Dodecahedron** — 12 pentagonal faces, golden ratio proportions
- **Icosahedron** — 20 triangular faces, closest to a sphere

**Technical implementation:**
- Pure 3D math from scratch (no libraries)
- Rotation matrices for X/Y/Z axes
- Perspective projection with distance scaling
- Depth sorting for proper edge layering
- Vertex glow with shadow blur

**Interaction:**
- Drag to rotate freely in 3D space
- Scroll to zoom in/out
- Auto-rotation when idle
- Color palette cycles on solid change
- Touch support for mobile

**Visual design:**
- Glowing edges with gradient coloring
- Depth-based opacity (distant edges fade)
- Gentle pulse animation
- 5 color schemes (blue, purple, cyan, pink, gold)

**Rams test:**

2. ✓ Useful — Interactive exploration of sacred geometry
3. ✓ Aesthetic — Glowing wireframes, depth cues
4. ✓ Understandable — Simple controls, clear feedback
5. ✓ Unobtrusive — Minimal UI, full immersion
6. ✓ Honest — Shows the mathematical structure
7. ✓ Long-lasting — Timeless geometric forms
8. ✓ Thorough — All 5 platonic solids, polished
9. ✓ Less design — No decoration, just geometry

**Result:** The 11th standalone piece. Sacred geometry, rendered luminous.
