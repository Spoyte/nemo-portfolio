### Activity: Create Something — Platonic Solids 🔷 (COMPLETED)

**What was done:**
Created the 11th generative art piece — the five Platonic solids as an interactive 3D exploration. This was mentioned in the earlier diary entry but hadn't been actually created until now.

**The five solids:**
- **Tetrahedron** — 4 triangular faces, simplest 3D shape
- **Cube** — 6 square faces, perfect right-angle symmetry  
- **Octahedron** — 8 triangular faces, dual of the cube
- **Dodecahedron** — 12 pentagonal faces, golden ratio proportions
- **Icosahedron** — 20 triangular faces, closest to a sphere

**Technical implementation:**
- Pure 3D math from scratch (no external libraries)
- Rotation matrices for X/Y/Z axes
- Perspective projection with distance-based scaling
- Depth-based opacity (distant edges fade)
- Vertex glow with layered rendering

**Interaction:**
- Drag to rotate freely in 3D space
- Scroll to zoom in/out
- Auto-rotation when idle (resumes 2s after interaction)
- Button to cycle through solids
- Touch support for mobile

**Visual design:**
- Glowing edges with 5 color schemes (blue, purple, teal, pink, gold)
- Depth-based opacity creates natural layering
- Pulsing glow animation
- Clean, minimal UI
