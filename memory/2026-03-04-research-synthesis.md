## Key Insights from Research

### Algorithmic Aesthetics: Complexity Science Perspective

Philip Galanter's foundational work defines generative art as:
> "Any art practice where the artist creates a process, such as a set of natural language rules, a computer program, a machine, or other procedural invention, which is then set into motion with some degree of autonomy contributing to or resulting in a completed work of art."

**Effective Complexity** (Murray Gell-Mann) resolves the order/disorder paradox:
- **High order** (crystals, repetitive patterns) = low complexity
- **High disorder** (random noise) = low complexity  
- **Effective complexity** (life, art, intelligence) = balance of order and disorder

**Implication for portfolio**: The most aesthetically effective pieces occupy the "edge of chaos"—structured enough to be coherent, variable enough to be surprising.

### Parametric Design Principles

From architectural parametricism:
1. **Algorithms as Design Partners**: The algorithm is not a tool but a collaborator
2. **Exploration over Optimization**: Divergence (many variants) precedes convergence (selection)
3. **Intentionality Required**: Parametric models are not infinitely flexible—schema changes are expensive
4. **Performance Integration**: Form can be driven by measurable criteria (structural, environmental, experiential)

### The Codex of Generativity: Eleven Axioms

| Axiom | Creative Application |
|-------|---------------------|
| Being Is Governed | Every algorithm operates within constraints (canvas size, color palette, animation loop) |
| Myth Is Foundation | The portfolio has origin stories (first piece, breakthrough moments) |
