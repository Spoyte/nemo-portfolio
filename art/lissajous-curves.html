Harmonic Presets

Lissajous figures are patterns formed when two perpendicular harmonic motions combine.

The parametric equations:

x = A·sin(a·t + δ)
y = B·sin(b·t)

When the frequency ratio is rational, the curve closes. When irrational, it never repeats.
