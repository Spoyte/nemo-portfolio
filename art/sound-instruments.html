🎧 Why Browser-Based Audio?

The Web Audio API is one of the most underappreciated browser capabilities. It offers sample-accurate scheduling, real-time synthesis, and spatial audio — all without plugins or downloads.

These instruments demonstrate what's possible when you treat the browser as a creative platform, not just a document viewer. Each one is a complete, self-contained musical instrument that loads instantly and works everywhere.

No accounts. No subscriptions. No app stores. Just sound, code, and curiosity.
